# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and considerate. Assume good intent, and disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults, personal attacks, or unwelcome sexual attention, in any form.
- Do not publish other people's private information.
- Accept that maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other
  contributions that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
place where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer
through GitHub: open the maintainer's profile at <https://github.com/debmalyaroy> and use
the contact options there, or use *Report content* on the comment or issue itself.
Reports are handled confidentially.
