Inkdown 1.3.3 makes the Back button and gesture on Android work the way they do in other apps.

| Platform | File | Status |

`SHA256SUMS.txt` lists the checksum of every file. Windows 1.0.1 and later update
themselves (*Help > Check for Updates*). The Android package installs over 1.0.3 and later.

## Back on Android

Until now Back only closed whatever was open on top. With nothing open it did nothing:
it did not leave editing, did not return to the document before, and never left the app.

Back now goes one step back with each press, in this order:

1. **It closes what is on top**: a menu sheet, a dialog, the side drawer, a picker, a
   picture shown full screen, or the search bar (in documents and in PDF files). The
   on-screen keyboard, when it is up, is put away first by the system, as everywhere.
2. **It leaves editing**: from Write or Markdown mode back to Read, for documents that
   have a reader.
3. **It returns to the document that was on screen before**, one document for each
   press. Back never closes a document.
4. **It leaves Inkdown** when there is nothing left to go back to. The app goes to the
   background with its documents open, as with the Home button.

The preview that some apps show while the Back gesture is in progress (a glimpse of the
home screen) is not shown yet.

Everything else is as in [1.3.2](v1.3.2.md).

## Testing for this release

- New scripted checks of the real window in the phone layout: Back closing a sheet, the
  search bar and a picker; editing to reading; back through three documents; leaving the
  app once there is nothing to go back to, without closing a document.
- All unit tests and the whole suite of UI checks on Windows, and in CI on Linux and macOS.
- **Not checked on a phone.** No device was connected: the Back button and the Back
  gesture themselves, and the step that puts the app in the background, still have to
  be confirmed on a device. The same goes for pinch and double tap from 1.3.2.
